Roblox Studio Setup Guide

Get ready to build your own 3D worlds and games!

Step 1 Download Roblox Studio

Roblox Studio is the essential tool for building Roblox games. It's free and available for Windows and Mac.

Step 2 Create a Roblox Account

If you don't have one, create a Roblox account. Make sure to keep your password safe!

Step 3 Launch & Explore

Open Roblox Studio and log in. Your teacher will guide you through the interface in your first session.
